## 🌟 About

**AI Gym Workout & Diet Planner** is an intelligent fitness companion that generates **personalized workout routines and nutrition plans** based on your body metrics, fitness level, equipment, goals, and dietary preferences.

Instead of generic cookie-cutter programs, it uses an AI model to adapt to **your** body and schedule — and it updates as you progress.

> 💡 **Perfect for:** Beginners who don't know where to start, intermediate lifters breaking plateaus, and anyone who wants structured training with proper nutrition.

## 🏆 Why This App

<div align="center">

| 💎 **Advantage** | 📝 **Description** |
|:---|:---|
| 🧠 **Truly Personalized** | Considers age, weight, height, activity level, injuries & goals |
| ⚡ **Instant Generation** | Complete 12-week plan in under 10 seconds |
| 🍽️ **Real Food Choices** | Plans use everyday ingredients, not obscure supplements |
| 🎯 **Adaptive** | Recalculates macros as your weight or goals change |
| 🛡️ **Safe by Design** | Warns on unsafe deficits, excessive volume, or injury-risk moves |
| 📱 **Mobile-Friendly** | Track workouts and meals from the gym floor |
